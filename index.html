06 — Sara Bendrick

Cal Poly architecture training. Mexican-American soil-and-plant inheritance.

Sara founded Sarita Landscapes in 2011 after a bachelor’s in landscape architecture from Cal Poly San Luis Obispo. The technical training met the family practice. Thirteen years of full design-build work across coastal San Diego since.

HGTV picked her up in 2014. Three shows since: I Hate My Yard, Lawn & Order, Build It Like Bendrick. STIHL named her a national spokesperson + Team STIHL ambassador in 2019. Quarto published Big Impact Landscaping in 2021 — 28 DIY projects every coastal homeowner should know.

The thread through all of it is the same: women in trades, real soil, real plants, owner-led practice. Sarita Landscapes is the firm; Big Impact is the book; HGTV is the broadcast; STIHL is the tools. One operator behind all four.
